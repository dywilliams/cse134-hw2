Why MathML Adoption Was Delayed Across Browsers

Although MathML has been part of the W3C specification since 1998, broad cross-browser adoption lagged for decades due to lack of consensus on typography rendering engines and high implementation complexity inside browser rendering cores. While Gecko (Firefox) supported MathML natively for years and WebKit (Safari) implemented it in 2016, Chromium (Chrome, Edge) removed its initial prototype and withheld full support. It was not until the standardized MathML Core initiative was finalized and subsequently shipped in Chromium 109 (January 2023) that true cross-browser baseline interoperability was finally achieved.
